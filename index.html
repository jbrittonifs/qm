Indices Rules

Multiplication

\( x^a \cdot x^b = x^{a+b} \)

Division

\( \dfrac{x^a}{x^b} = x^{a-b} \)

Power of a Power

\( (x^a)^b = x^{ab} \)

Zero Exponent

\( x^0 = 1 \quad (x \neq 0) \)

Negative Exponent

\( x^{-a} = \dfrac{1}{x^a} \)

Fractional Exponent

\( x^{a/b} = \sqrt[b]{x^a} = \left(\sqrt[b]{x}\right)^a \)

Log & Exponential Facts

Product Rule

\( \ln(ab) = \ln a + \ln b \)

Quotient Rule

\( \ln\!\left(\dfrac{a}{b}\right) = \ln a - \ln b \)

Power Rule

\( \ln(a^n) = n \ln a \)

Key Values

\( \ln e = 1, \quad \ln 1 = 0 \)

Inverse Relationships

\( e^{\ln x} = x, \quad \ln(e^x) = x \)

Exponential Growth

\( e^{a+b} = e^a \cdot e^b, \quad e^{-a} = \dfrac{1}{e^a} \)

Differentiation Rules

Power Rule

\( \dfrac{d}{dx}\left[x^n\right] = n x^{n-1} \)

Constant Multiple

\( \dfrac{d}{dx}\left[cf(x)\right] = c\,f'(x) \)

Sum / Difference

\( \dfrac{d}{dx}\left[f \pm g\right] = f'(x) \pm g'(x) \)

Product Rule

\( \dfrac{d}{dx}\left[f \cdot g\right] = f'g + fg' \)

Quotient Rule

\( \dfrac{d}{dx}\!\left[\dfrac{f}{g}\right] = \dfrac{f'g - fg'}{g^2} \)

Chain Rule

\( \dfrac{d}{dx}\left[f(g(x))\right] = f'(g(x)) \cdot g'(x) \)

Exponential

\( \dfrac{d}{dx}\left[e^{f(x)}\right] = f'(x) \cdot e^{f(x)} \)

Logarithm

\( \dfrac{d}{dx}\left[\ln f(x)\right] = \dfrac{f'(x)}{f(x)} \)

Stationary Points of 3D Functions

For a function \(f(x, y)\), stationary points occur where \(f_x = 0\) and \(f_y = 0\). To classify them, compute the Hessian determinant:

Hessian

\( H = f_{xx} \cdot f_{yy} - (f_{xy})^2 \)

Maximum

\( H > 0 \) and \( f_{xx} < 0 \)

Minimum

\( H > 0 \) and \( f_{xx} > 0 \)

Saddle Point

\( H < 0 \)

Inconclusive

\( H = 0 \)

Concavity & Convexity

Concave (curves down)

\( f''(x) < 0 \)

Convex (curves up)

\( f''(x) > 0 \)

Inflection Point

\( f''(x) = 0 \) (and sign change)
